Dincharya – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026  ·  App: Dincharya (Android, com.xmenappstudio.dincharya)

In short: Dincharya has no account, no login and no developer server that stores your personal information. Your city, name, birth details, favourites and progress stay in the app’s storage on your own phone. The only third party that receives data about you is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the app free. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK.

1. What we collect

1.1 No account, no sign-in

Dincharya does not ask you to create an account. There is no Google Sign-In, no email/password sign-up, no phone-number verification and no social login. We never receive your email address, phone number, password or contacts.

1.2 Information you enter, stored only on your device

The following is written to the app’s private storage on your phone (Android app data) so the app remembers your routine. It is not transmitted to us or to Supabase, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

  • Profile details you choose to enter — an optional display name, your rashi, the city you select for panchang calculations (with that city’s latitude/longitude), and your app language.
  • Kundli / birth details — if you fill the optional Kundli form: name, date of birth, time of birth and place of birth. These values are saved on the device only, and the chart is computed on the device. The app has no code path that uploads, syncs or backs them up to any server.
  • Preferences — notification on/off and reminder time, per-category reminder toggles, animation and sound settings, Festival Mode preference, and premium-unlock state.
  • Your activity in the app — favourites, daily-routine checklist progress, puja guide progress, reading progress, play counts, streak/visit history, join date and simple in-app counters (books read, mantras viewed, wallpapers set).
  • Cached content — downloaded wallpaper/audio files and the computed panchang calendar for the coming weeks, kept in the app’s cache so the app works offline.
Location: Dincharya does not request or use your device location. You pick your city manually from a list built into the app; only that city’s coordinates are stored. The app declares no location permission, so it cannot read GPS.

1.3 Information collected automatically by third parties

  • Google AdMob — the advertising SDK may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language), and ad impressions/clicks. See section 3.
  • Supabase Storage (our media host) — when the app downloads a bhajan, aarti, wallpaper or temple image, Supabase’s servers log standard technical data such as IP address, user-agent and the file requested. No name, birth details or app activity is sent with it.
  • YouTube (Google) — some bhajans and temple videos are YouTube links. The video player is only loaded if you tap play, and may then collect data under Google’s policies.

1.4 What we do not collect

  • No contacts, SMS, call logs or calendar data.
  • No photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No precise or approximate GPS location.
  • No health, financial or payment information — the app has no payments or in-app purchases.
  • No analytics events, session recordings or crash reports — the app ships no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app’s features on your device: calculating the daily panchang, muhurat and festival themes for your chosen city; showing your routine checklist, streak, favourites and reading progress; computing a Kundli chart; setting a wallpaper you selected.
  • To deliver reminders: scheduling the optional daily notifications locally on your phone at the time you choose.
  • To deliver media: downloading the audio, wallpaper and image files you open, then caching them so they play offline.
  • To show ads: Google AdMob serves interstitial and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are shown only when you tap to unlock an optional wallpaper/ringtone pack; there is no paid content.
  • To keep the app working and lawful: diagnosing delivery problems at the hosting layer, and complying with applicable law and store policies.

We do not sell personal data, we do not build advertising profiles of you ourselves, and we do not use your Kundli or birth details for ads.

5. Data security

  • Everything you enter is kept in the app’s private, sandboxed storage, protected by Android’s app isolation and, on modern devices, full-disk/file-based encryption.
  • All network traffic (media downloads, ads, video) uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Because there is no account and no developer database of user records, there is no server-side store of your profile, Kundli details or activity that could be breached.
  • Media and ad requests necessarily expose your IP address to Supabase, Google and YouTube, as with any app that loads content from the internet.
  • No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; a rooted or compromised device can expose locally stored app data.

6. Data retention & deletion

  • On your device: your data is kept only as long as the app is installed. Delete it at any time by uninstalling Dincharya, or via Android Settings → Apps → Dincharya → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling also removes cached audio and wallpapers.
  • On our side: we hold no server-side copy of your profile, birth details, favourites or activity, so there is nothing for us to delete — but if you want confirmation, or you believe some data about you reached us, email wahid1874254@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete anything found, normally within 30 days.
  • Advertising data: data held by Google for ad delivery and measurement is controlled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or through Google’s privacy controls. Uninstalling Dincharya does not delete data Google already holds.
  • Hosting logs: Supabase’s technical request logs are retained for a short period under its standard policy and are not linked to an identity.

7. Children

Dincharya is a general-audience devotional app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13, please do not use the app. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, contact us at wahid1874254@gmail.com and we will delete it.
